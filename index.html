Common questions

Frequently asked questions

I've been handling my case myself — is this for me?

Yes — that's exactly who this is built for. Most people I talk to have already been navigating USCIS forms, deadlines, or a removal case on their own. I review what you've filed or received, point out anything that needs attention, and lay out what comes next.

What do I get for the $450?

Before we meet, I personally review the documents you upload. Then we spend 45 minutes on Zoom going through your case: what's working, what the red flags are, and the specific next steps I'd take in your position. You leave with a clear plan — not a sales pitch.

Why isn't the consultation free?

Free consultations are usually a screening call to sell you full representation. This is different: the consultation is the service. You're paying for an experienced attorney's focused review of your actual documents and a working session on your actual case — and you stay in control of it afterward.

I'm in immigration court (removal proceedings). Can you help?

Yes — I have removal defense and federal litigation experience, and I can review your notices, explain what's happening in plain English, and lay out your options. If you have a hearing date coming up, book as soon as possible; deadlines in these matters move quickly.

Can we really do this entirely over Zoom?

Yes. The practice is built around remote consultations, serving clients anywhere in Florida. All you need is a phone or computer with internet access. Consultations are conducted in English; interpreter arrangements are available on request.

What if my case needs more than a consultation?

I'll tell you plainly. If your matter needs a full-representation attorney, you'll hear that directly during our session, along with where I'd point you. No pressure, no bait-and-switch — the session's job is to give you the truth about your case.
